Privacy Policy

The short version — plain English

  • We never collect, transmit, or store your clipboard data on any server
  • Everything stays on your device, always
  • We don't sell your data — not now, not ever
  • We don't use analytics, trackers, or third-party services
  • You can delete all stored data at any time from the extension popup

1. Who we are

Clipboard Guardian is a browser extension built to protect users from clipboard hijacking — a technique used by malicious websites to secretly modify the text you copy. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles your data.

2. What data the extension accesses

To detect clipboard tampering, the extension needs to:

  • Read the text you select on a webpage — to know what you intended to copy
  • Read your clipboard contents immediately after a copy action — to compare it against what you selected
  • Read the URL of the current page — so the event log can show you which site triggered an alert
This access happens entirely on your device. None of this information is ever sent anywhere — not to us, not to any third party, not to any server of any kind.

3. What data is stored — and where

The extension stores a local history of clipboard events (up to 100 entries) using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This means:

  • Data is saved only on your device, in your browser's local storage
  • It is never synced to Chrome Sync, Google's servers, or any external service
  • Each stored entry contains: whether tampering was detected, the reason, a short preview of the copied text (up to 200 characters), the page URL, and a timestamp
  • You can clear all stored data at any time by clicking "Clear all" inside the extension popup
  • Uninstalling the extension permanently deletes all stored data

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not transmit any data to external servers
  • We do not collect personal information of any kind
  • We do not use analytics tools, crash reporters, or usage tracking
  • We do not use cookies or any tracking mechanisms
  • We do not sell, rent, share, or monetise your data in any way
  • We do not access your clipboard outside of a user-initiated copy action

5. Permissions explained

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions, and here is exactly why each one is needed:

  • clipboardRead — required to read clipboard contents after a copy event, so we can compare it to what you selected and detect tampering
  • storage — required to save the local event history so you can review it in the popup
  • notifications — reserved for a future optional feature to show system-level alerts; currently unused
  • host permissions (all URLs) — required so the detection script can run on any website you visit, since clipboard hijacking can happen on any site

We request only what is necessary. If a permission is not needed for the core protection feature, we do not request it.

6. Children's privacy

Clipboard Guardian does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data leaves your device, there is nothing to collect.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the new version will be published at this URL and the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. We will never introduce data collection or reduce your privacy protections without clear notice.

8. Your rights

Since we do not collect or hold any personal data on our end, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. All data is yours, on your device, under your full control.
